
Documentation Feedback ~ Deliverable #3
Software Development Plan [SDP]
CMSI 4071 Fall 2026
=======================================

INTRODUCTION/DELIVERABLES SECTION:
   Assessment: Partially Complete
      Strengths
         • The project vision and intended functionality are clearly
           described.
         • Major course deliverables are identified through the Class
           Schedule section.
         • The project roadmap shows how functionality will be
           delivered incrementally.
         • Development priorities are clearly established.
      Areas for Improvement
         • The SDP does not contain a dedicated Deliverables section.
         • Project-specific deliverables are not defined in detail.
         • Acceptance criteria are largely absent.
         • Ownership for deliverables is not assigned.
      Suggestions
         1. Add a dedicated Deliverables section listing every major
            technical, documentation, testing, presentation, and final
            delivery artifact with descriptions and completion dates.
         2. Define measurable acceptance criteria and assign a primary
            owner for each deliverable.

PROJECT RESOURCES SECTION:
   Assessment: Very Good
      Strengths
         • Hardware and software resources are clearly separated.
         • Resource descriptions explain how each item will be used.
         • Hardware planning supports both early software prototypes
           and later hardware integration.
         • Software technologies are appropriate for the project's
           objectives.
         • Resource planning reflects awareness of implementation
           needs and project constraints.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Hardware specifications are somewhat minimal.
         • Resource availability and contingency planning are not
           discussed.
      Suggestions
         1. Include minimum hardware specifications and expected
            performance requirements for development and deployment.
         2. Document backup plans for critical resources such as the
            vision service, Raspberry Pi hardware, and cloud services.

PROJECT ORGANIZATION SECTION:
   Assessment: Good
      Strengths
         • Major work areas are identified clearly.
         • The team emphasizes collaboration and peer review.
         • Development, testing, and documentation activities are
            recognized as important responsibilities.
         • The development process is described sufficiently to show
            how work will be coordinated.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Individual responsibilities are not assigned.
         • No organizational chart or reporting structure is
           provided.
         • Communication procedures are informal.
      Suggestions
         1. Assign each team member ownership of specific technical
            areas and project responsibilities.
         2. Add an organizational chart and define meeting schedules,
            decision-making procedures, and conflict-resolution
            processes.

PROJECT SCHEDULE SECTION:
   Assessment: Very Good
      Strengths
         • Includes a GANTT-style schedule spanning the remainder of
           the semester.
         • Provides a Task/Resource Table linking activities to
           resources and target dates.
         • Includes a Class Schedule section that maps project work to
           course deliverables.
         • The schedule reflects a reasonable progression from
           software development to hardware integration and testing.
         • Risk awareness is reflected in the planning approach.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Task dependencies are not explicitly documented.
         • Critical-path activities are not identified.
         • Resource assignments are not tied to specific team members.
      Suggestions
         1. Add task dependencies and identify critical-path
            activities that could affect final delivery.
         2. Assign responsible team members to major tasks and include
            effort estimates for improved schedule tracking.
