COMMENTS:
The architectural SDD presents a coherent high-level architecture that is
largely consistent with the requirements.  The document correctly preserves
the major CSC decomposition and provides a reasonable description of how
image capture, recognition, inventory management, persistence,
notifications, and recipe recommendations interact.

The document is strongest in maintaining traceability to the SRS, preserving
the required review-and-confirm workflow, and presenting a clear end-to-end
system flow.  Its main weakness is that it often describes the current
implementation rather than the target architecture required by the SRS.
Several sections are relatively brief and do not fully discuss architectural
responsibilities, interfaces, quality attributes, performance considerations,
or failure handling.

The design demonstrates solid architectural thinking.  The strongest aspects
are traceability, preservation of the review-and-confirm model, and clear
architectural diagrams.

The primary improvement opportunities involve expanding architectural detail
for the Data Management, Notification, Expiration Management, and Recipe
Suggestions CSCs, adding more discussion of performance requirements, and
strengthening descriptions of interfaces and failure handling.

Overall Rating: Good Architectural SDD

Good work overall!
